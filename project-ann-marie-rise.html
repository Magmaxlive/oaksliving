The Lifestyle

Living in Flat Bush

Life at Annmarie Rise is defined by the best of modern suburban living. Flat Bush is a vibrant, master-planned community where families enjoy wide streets, contemporary architecture, and an abundance of green spaces. Weekend mornings start with a stroll through Barry Curtis Park - one of Auckland's largest urban parks - while afternoons can be spent exploring the cafes and boutiques of Ormiston Town Centre.

Flat Bush is one of Auckland's largest master-planned residential communities, positioned in the south-east between Botany Downs, Ormiston, and Manukau. With a population of over 41,000 residents and a median household income of $96,500, Flat Bush is a thriving, diverse community with excellent infrastructure and amenities.

Local Area Knowledge

Your Neighbourhood

Everything you need is close at hand. From quality schools to green spaces, shopping, and transport - discover what makes this neighbourhood special.

Schools

  • Ormiston Primary School Primary (Decile 8)
  • Te Uho o Te Nikau Primary School Primary
  • Ormiston Junior College Years 7-10
  • Ormiston Senior College Years 11-13

Parks & Recreation

  • Barry Curtis Park 94-hectare urban park with sports fields and trails.
  • Murphys Bush Reserve Native bush reserve with walking tracks.
  • Flat Bush Neighbourhood Reserves Pocket parks and playgrounds throughout the suburb.

Shopping & Dining

  • Ormiston Town Centre Supermarkets, retail, cinema, and amenities.
  • Botany Town Centre Large shopping and dining destination.
  • Westfield Manukau City Regional retail and entertainment options.

Transport

  • Te Irirangi Drive & Ormiston Road Bus Links Frequent bus services.
  • Eastern Busway Connecting Botany and Panmure (under development).
  • SH1 & SH20 Access Approximately 30-45 minutes to Auckland CBD by car.
