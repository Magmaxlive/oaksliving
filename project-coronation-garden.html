About the Development

Coronation Garden

Coronation Garden is a premier residential development situated in the heart of Māngere Bridge. Designed to blend modern urban living with expansive green spaces, this development offers a balance of quality, connectivity, and community.

Māngere Bridge, South Auckland
The Lifestyle

Living in Māngere Bridge

Residents enjoy the charming village atmosphere of Māngere Bridge, with easy access to coastal walkways, local cafes, and the stunning Kiwi Esplanade. Each residence at Coronation Garden is crafted with Oaks Living's signature emphasis on quality and functionality.

Large-scale infrastructure pathways integrated into the community plan ensure a seamless transition between private homes and vibrant shared spaces.
