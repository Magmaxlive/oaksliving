About the Development

Wainui Terraces

Wainui Terraces is a collection of architecturally designed homes in the rapidly developing area of Silverdale. These residences provide the perfect blend of modern convenience and high-quality craftsmanship.

Silverdale, North Auckland
The Lifestyle

Coastal Hills & Gateway Convenience

Silverdale is one of Auckland's fastest-growing regions, offering a unique mix of retail hubs, top-tier schools, and stunning regional parks. Wainui Terraces puts you at the center of it all.

The homes feature open-plan living, designer kitchens, and refined finishes, providing a canvas for a modern lifestyle.
