About Oaks Living

Rooted in Land. Designed for Life’s Transitions.

Master-planned communities across Auckland, Queenstown, and key growth areas throughout Aotearoa.

Who we are

Our Story

Oaks Living is a New Zealand–based property developer dedicated to creating master-planned communities across Auckland, Queenstown, and key growth areas throughout Aotearoa.

We shape neighbourhoods and mixed-use townships that thoughtfully integrate residential lots, apartments, retail precincts, and refined urban design — delivering places where people live, connect, invest, and grow.

Our developments are driven by long-term thinking, disciplined investment, and institutional-grade stewardship — contributing lasting value to the cities we shape.

We recognise that both land and life move in cycles. Families evolve. Markets shift. Communities mature. Generations transition.

Oaks Living designs with this awareness — creating resilient environments that adapt over time while preserving long-term value.

We build for change.

We plan for continuity.

We deliver communities designed to endure beyond cycles.

How we work

Our Philosophy

Like farming, enduring value is cultivated. It requires patience, stewardship, and a deep respect for the land.

Every development is grounded in thoughtful planning, disciplined strategy, and long-term intent — honouring both the land itself and the lives that will unfold upon it.

Who we serve

Designed for Every
Stage of Life

We create homes and investment platforms designed to serve every stage of life — and every stakeholder within it.

  • For first-home buyers taking their first step
  • For families seeking space, connection, and stability
  • For builders and developers expanding their vision
  • For retirees seeking comfort, dignity, and calm
  • For investors planting seeds for the future

What drives us

Our Purpose

Creating enduring communities that balance land, life, and long-term value.

We exist to harmonise community, capital, and culture — creating places where people can live, invest, and build generational wealth. Every development is designed with permanence in mind and built to stand the test of time.

Our Vision

Homes and investment platforms for every stage of life.

We envision a future where every New Zealander and investor has access to thoughtfully planned homes and property opportunities aligned with their evolving needs, aspirations, and long-term wealth creation goals.

We aspire to create communities built on strong foundations — resilient through market cycles and enduring across generations.

Our Mission

Strategic land. Disciplined development. Enduring performance.

We deliver integrated development solutions through:

  • Strategic land acquisition and structured rezoning
  • Master-planned communities with disciplined phasing
  • Infrastructure delivered alongside large-scale development
  • Mixed-use environments that create self-sustaining precincts
  • Institutional-grade investment structures including Build-to-Rent platforms
  • Genuine consultation with iwi to uphold the mauri of the land

We plan with rigour, build with intention, and structure for long-term resilience.
