About the Development

The Reach

The Reach is a collection of 38 three-storey terraced residences at 53–65 Frankton Road, Queenstown, developed by Wildridge Projects and designed by AWA Architects. Stepping down the hillside above Lake Wakatipu, this enclave offers three- and four-bedroom homes, each with its own parking.

With the town centre within an easy walk, it offers a rare opportunity to own in central Queenstown, whether as a place to keep for yourself or to share with others while you're away.

53–65 Frankton Road, Queenstown
The Lifestyle

Living in Queenstown

Life at The Reach follows the seasons. Winter brings blue-sky days on the slopes of Coronet Peak and The Remarkables, while summer moves to the lake, with twilight lasting past nine. Autumn turns the valleys gold, and spring can mean skiing in the morning and golf in the afternoon.

Each home is designed for easy living, with the kitchen, dining and living areas on the top floor, where the deck extends towards the lake. There are no lawns to mow or gardens to maintain, so you can simply arrive and enjoy it.

Some residences also include a self-contained studio at entry level, with its own key, for guests, older children or tenants.

Local Area Knowledge

Your Neighbourhood

Queenstown is one of New Zealand's most sought-after lifestyle destinations, with the lake, mountains, and vineyards on your doorstep.

On Foot

  • Queenstown Waterfront Lakeside restaurants and strolls.
  • Town Centre Galleries, spas and boutiques.
  • Queenstown Gardens Rose beds and century-old trees.

Mountains & Trails

  • Coronet Peak Winter skiing.
  • The Remarkables Winter skiing, on the range south-east of town.
  • Walking & Cycle Trails Hundreds of kilometres to explore, including the Shotover Gorge Trail.

Wine, Golf & Dining

  • Cellar Doors Pinot noir country within half an hour's drive.
  • Queenstown Golf Club Golf with a backdrop of lake and mountains.
  • Dining Amisfield, Essence, Toast & Oak, The Woolshed at Ayrburn, The Dishery and The Boatshed.

Day Trips

  • Arrowtown Historic gold-rush streets, just beyond the wine country.
  • Glenorchy At the head of Lake Wakatipu.
  • Wānaka Over the Crown Range.
  • National Parks Mount Aspiring and Fiordland.

Transport

  • Frankton Road Easy access, moments from the town centre.
  • Airport Ten minutes from the town centre.
  • Direct Flights Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and the Gold Coast.
Project Story

Find The Reach

The Reach is positioned at 53–65 Frankton Road, overlooking Kelvin Peninsula, Lake Wakatipu and the ranges beyond. AWA Architects arranged the homes in eight terraced rows that follow the contours of the hillside, with each row sitting lower than the one behind to maximise sightlines.

For Wildridge Projects, The Reach is part of a new chapter of quality-built developments across the Southern Lakes. The team brings more than 10 years of experience on major North Island projects, and its current portfolio includes an 850-lot master-planned development in Auckland.
